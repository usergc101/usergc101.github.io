The phone proves itself. You watch.

Knowfone does not just read what a phone says about itself, because a modified phone can say anything. Wherever it can, one part tests another and you see, hear or feel the same thing.

Identity

Processor, graphics, memory, screen and storage compared with the model it claims to be. Copies dressed as famous models stand out.

Security

The phone's security chip signs its answer, checked up to Google's root. A tampered system cannot forge it.

Touch screen

Follow a light with one finger across every 7 mm of the glass. Dead strips show up on the map.

Display

Full-screen colour pages for dead pixels, lines and burn-in, and the real refresh rate.

Speakers and mics

The speaker plays a sweep and the microphone hears it. You hear it too.

Cameras

Every lens opens and focuses. The flash fires and the camera sees it.

Battery

Health, charge and how the voltage holds up under load.

Processor and memory

A stress test that checks it stays stable and as fast as this model should be.

Storage

Writes real data and reads it back, to prove the size it claims and find errors.

Sensors

A tilt game, the compass and the sensor that turns the screen off near your ear.

Buttons and port

Volume and power keys, the charging port, and the vibration motor, felt by the motion sensor.

Wireless and SIM

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS satellites and the SIM.

What no app can check

Knowfone says so in every report, so you know what to check by hand.

Past water damage or repairs inside

Open-and-look work for a repair shop. Knowfone tests whether each part works today.

Stolen, blocked or on a contract

Dial *#06#, compare the IMEI with the box, and check it with your network. Ask the seller to sign out of their Google account in front of you.

Exactly how long it will last

Battery health and a stress test are strong hints, not a promise.

Free, with fair ads

Ads pay for Knowfone. They follow four rules, written into the app's code.

Never during a test

Ads use the processor, network and battery. During a test they would make the numbers less true, so the app blocks them.

Never before your answer

No pop-ups between you and your result.

About the report, not about you

Ads are picked by what is on the screen, like a worn battery, not by who you are.

One optional ad, for sellers

A short ad unlocks the seller certificate. You choose to watch it, and you can skip it.

Questions

Is Knowfone free?

Yes. Every test and every answer is free. Knowfone shows a few ads after your answer, never during a test.

Can it tell if a phone is a clone?

It compares the processor, graphics, memory, screen, storage and cameras with the model the phone claims to be, and asks the security chip for a signed answer checked up to Google's root. A copy that only looks like a famous model usually fails these checks. Knowfone tells you what it measured, for example "not the processor this model uses".

Does it upload my data?

No. There is no account and your reports stay on the phone. A report leaves the phone only when you share it yourself. See the privacy policy.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones running Android 8.0 or newer, from every brand. Parts a phone does not have are marked "couldn't test", never as broken.

Whose phone do I install it on?

On the phone you are about to buy. Ask the seller to install it, or install it yourself from this page while you hold the phone.

Can it see water damage or tell if a phone is stolen?

No app can. Knowfone says so in every report and lists what to check by hand. Compare the IMEI (dial *#06#) with the box and ask your network operator to check it is not blocked.
