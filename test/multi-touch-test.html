Multi-touch test

Free. Runs in your phone's browser. Nothing to install, nothing uploaded.

Most phones follow 5 to 10 fingers at once. Here each finger gets its own coloured dot. Dots that jump, flicker or vanish while your fingers stay still point to a failing touch layer.

All 10 phone tests

Open this page on the phone you want to test. Press Back or the close button to stop at any time.

How to do it

  1. Tap Start.
  2. Put one finger down, then add more, up to all ten.
  3. Hold still for a few seconds and watch the dots, then slide your fingers around.

What the result means

  • Every finger has a steady dot: multi-touch works.
  • Dots jump or vanish while you hold still: the touch layer is failing or the screen was badly replaced.
  • Two fingers close together merge into one: normal on many phones when fingers touch each other.

Questions

How many touch points should a phone have?

Most Android phones and iPhones follow at least 5 fingers, and many follow 10. A browser can report fewer than the phone supports, so a count of 5 here is fine.

Why do I need multi-touch?

Zooming with two fingers, many games and some keyboards need it. A phone that loses a second finger often has a damaged touch layer.
