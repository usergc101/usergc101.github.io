Is my Samsung Galaxy S24 original?

Updated October 2026. Real specs from the sources below, and how to check a used Samsung Galaxy S24 in about 10 minutes.

Short answer: A real Samsung Galaxy S24 has either the Exynos 2400 or the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy, depending on the version, 8 GB of RAM, 128 GB, 256 GB or 512 GB of storage, a 6.2-inch AMOLED screen (1080 x 2340, up to 120 Hz) and a 4000 mAh battery. Its model code is SM-S921B, SM-S921N, SM-S921E, SM-S921Q, SM-S921U, SM-S921U1, SM-S921W, SM-S9210 (and other SM-S921... versions). A copy can show the right name, but it cannot change the processor, the real storage or the screen.

How to check a used Samsung Galaxy S24

  1. Read the model code. Open Settings, About phone, Model number. It must say SM-S921B, SM-S921N, SM-S921E, SM-S921Q, SM-S921U, SM-S921U1, SM-S921W, SM-S9210 (and other SM-S921... versions). Copies often copy this name too, so this is only the first check.
  2. Check the processor. It must be either the Exynos 2400 or the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy, depending on the version. Copies run on a cheaper chip and sometimes rename it, so look at the number of cores and the graphics chip too. The Knowfone app does this for you and compares every part with this exact model.
  3. Do the black screen test. The real Samsung Galaxy S24 has an AMOLED screen: open the black colour of the screen test in a dark room. It must be completely black. A grey glow means an LCD, which this model never had.
  4. Measure the refresh rate. Set Settings, Display to the highest refresh rate, then run the refresh rate test with a finger moving on the screen. The real phone reaches about 120 Hz.
  5. Prove the storage. Settings shows 128 GB, 256 GB or 512 GB on a real Samsung Galaxy S24. Copies can show any number; only writing data and reading it back proves the real size (how to check fake storage).
  6. Check the battery. The real battery holds 4000 mAh when new. See how to check battery health.
  7. Test the rest. Dial *#0*#: on many Galaxy phones it opens a test menu for the screen colours, touch grid, speaker, vibration and sensors (some versions block it). Samsung Members, Phone diagnostics, is the official alternative.

Questions

What processor does the Samsung Galaxy S24 have?

The Samsung Galaxy S24 uses either the Exynos 2400 or the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy, depending on the version. A phone sold as a Samsung Galaxy S24 with a different processor is not one.

How much RAM and storage does the Samsung Galaxy S24 have?

The Samsung Galaxy S24 comes with 8 GB of RAM and 128 GB, 256 GB or 512 GB of storage.

What is the model number of the Samsung Galaxy S24?

The Samsung Galaxy S24 reports the model code SM-S921B, SM-S921N, SM-S921E, SM-S921Q, SM-S921U, SM-S921U1, SM-S921W, SM-S9210 (and other SM-S921... versions). You find it in Settings, About phone, Model number. Copies often copy this name too, so check the processor and storage as well.

Does the Samsung Galaxy S24 have an AMOLED screen?

Yes. The Samsung Galaxy S24 has a 6.2-inch AMOLED screen that runs at up to 120 Hz. In a dark room a black picture looks completely black on it.
