Is my Infinix Hot 40 original?

Updated October 2026. Real specs from the sources below, and how to check a used Infinix Hot 40 in about 10 minutes.

Short answer: A real Infinix Hot 40 has the MediaTek Helio G88, 8 GB of RAM, 128 GB or 256 GB of storage, a 6.78-inch LCD screen (1080 x 2460, up to 90 Hz) and a 5000 mAh battery. Its model code is Infinix X6836. A copy can show the right name, but it cannot change the processor, the real storage or the screen.

How to check a used Infinix Hot 40

  1. Read the model code. Open Settings, About phone, Model. It must say Infinix X6836. Copies often copy this name too, so this is only the first check.
  2. Check the processor. It must be the MediaTek Helio G88. Copies run on a cheaper chip and sometimes rename it, so look at the number of cores and the graphics chip too. The Knowfone app does this for you and compares every part with this exact model.
  3. Check the screen. The real Infinix Hot 40 has an LCD at 1080 x 2460 pixels. Run the screen colour test for dots, lines and stains.
  4. Measure the refresh rate. Set Settings, Display to the highest refresh rate, then run the refresh rate test with a finger moving on the screen. The real phone reaches about 90 Hz.
  5. Prove the storage. Settings shows 128 GB or 256 GB on a real Infinix Hot 40. Copies can show any number; only writing data and reading it back proves the real size (how to check fake storage).
  6. Check the battery. The real battery holds 5000 mAh when new. See how to check battery health.
  7. Test the rest. Most Tecno and Infinix models have no public test code (see phone test codes by brand). Our online phone test checks the screen, touch, speakers, microphone, cameras and sensors on any phone without a code.

Questions

What processor does the Infinix Hot 40 have?

The Infinix Hot 40 uses the MediaTek Helio G88. A phone sold as a Infinix Hot 40 with a different processor is not one.

How much RAM and storage does the Infinix Hot 40 have?

The Infinix Hot 40 comes with 8 GB of RAM and 128 GB or 256 GB of storage.

What is the model number of the Infinix Hot 40?

The Infinix Hot 40 reports the model code Infinix X6836. You find it in Settings, About phone, Model. Copies often copy this name too, so check the processor and storage as well.

Does the Infinix Hot 40 have an AMOLED screen?

No. The Infinix Hot 40 has a 6.78-inch LCD screen that runs at up to 90 Hz. An LCD glows slightly grey on a black picture in the dark; that is normal for this model.
