Is my Tecno Camon 30 original?

Updated October 2026. Real specs from the sources below, and how to check a used Tecno Camon 30 in about 10 minutes.

Short answer: A real Tecno Camon 30 has the MediaTek Helio G99 Ultimate, 8 or 12 GB of RAM, 256 GB of storage, a 6.78-inch AMOLED screen (1080 x 2436, up to 120 Hz) and a 5000 mAh battery. Its model code is TECNO CL6, TECNO CL6k, TECNO CL6s. A copy can show the right name, but it cannot change the processor, the real storage or the screen.

How to check a used Tecno Camon 30

  1. Read the model code. Open Settings, About phone, Model. It must say TECNO CL6, TECNO CL6k, TECNO CL6s. Copies often copy this name too, so this is only the first check.
  2. Check the processor. It must be the MediaTek Helio G99 Ultimate. Copies run on a cheaper chip and sometimes rename it, so look at the number of cores and the graphics chip too. The Knowfone app does this for you and compares every part with this exact model.
  3. Do the black screen test. The real Tecno Camon 30 has an AMOLED screen: open the black colour of the screen test in a dark room. It must be completely black. A grey glow means an LCD, which this model never had.
  4. Measure the refresh rate. Set Settings, Display to the highest refresh rate, then run the refresh rate test with a finger moving on the screen. The real phone reaches about 120 Hz.
  5. Prove the storage. Settings shows 256 GB on a real Tecno Camon 30. Copies can show any number; only writing data and reading it back proves the real size (how to check fake storage).
  6. Check the battery. The real battery holds 5000 mAh when new. See how to check battery health.
  7. Test the rest. Most Tecno and Infinix models have no public test code (see phone test codes by brand). Our online phone test checks the screen, touch, speakers, microphone, cameras and sensors on any phone without a code.

Questions

What processor does the Tecno Camon 30 have?

The Tecno Camon 30 uses the MediaTek Helio G99 Ultimate. A phone sold as a Tecno Camon 30 with a different processor is not one.

How much RAM and storage does the Tecno Camon 30 have?

The Tecno Camon 30 comes with 8 or 12 GB of RAM and 256 GB of storage.

What is the model number of the Tecno Camon 30?

The Tecno Camon 30 reports the model code TECNO CL6, TECNO CL6k, TECNO CL6s. You find it in Settings, About phone, Model. Copies often copy this name too, so check the processor and storage as well.

Does the Tecno Camon 30 have an AMOLED screen?

Yes. The Tecno Camon 30 has a 6.78-inch AMOLED screen that runs at up to 120 Hz. In a dark room a black picture looks completely black on it.
