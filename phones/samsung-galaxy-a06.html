Is my Samsung Galaxy A06 original?

Updated October 2026. Real specs from the sources below, and how to check a used Samsung Galaxy A06 in about 10 minutes.

Short answer: A real Samsung Galaxy A06 has the MediaTek Helio G85, 4 or 6 GB of RAM, 64 GB or 128 GB of storage, a 6.7-inch LCD screen (720 x 1600, up to 60 Hz) and a 5000 mAh battery. Its model code is SM-A065F, SM-A065M (and other SM-A065... versions). A copy can show the right name, but it cannot change the processor, the real storage or the screen.

How to check a used Samsung Galaxy A06

  1. Read the model code. Open Settings, About phone, Model number. It must say SM-A065F, SM-A065M (and other SM-A065... versions). Copies often copy this name too, so this is only the first check.
  2. Check the processor. It must be the MediaTek Helio G85. Copies run on a cheaper chip and sometimes rename it, so look at the number of cores and the graphics chip too. The Knowfone app does this for you and compares every part with this exact model.
  3. Check the screen. The real Samsung Galaxy A06 has an LCD at 720 x 1600 pixels. Run the screen colour test for dots, lines and stains.
  4. Prove the storage. Settings shows 64 GB or 128 GB on a real Samsung Galaxy A06. Copies can show any number; only writing data and reading it back proves the real size (how to check fake storage).
  5. Check the battery. The real battery holds 5000 mAh when new. See how to check battery health.
  6. Test the rest. Dial *#0*#: on many Galaxy phones it opens a test menu for the screen colours, touch grid, speaker, vibration and sensors (some versions block it). Samsung Members, Phone diagnostics, is the official alternative.

Questions

What processor does the Samsung Galaxy A06 have?

The Samsung Galaxy A06 uses the MediaTek Helio G85. A phone sold as a Samsung Galaxy A06 with a different processor is not one.

How much RAM and storage does the Samsung Galaxy A06 have?

The Samsung Galaxy A06 comes with 4 or 6 GB of RAM and 64 GB or 128 GB of storage.

What is the model number of the Samsung Galaxy A06?

The Samsung Galaxy A06 reports the model code SM-A065F, SM-A065M (and other SM-A065... versions). You find it in Settings, About phone, Model number. Copies often copy this name too, so check the processor and storage as well.

Does the Samsung Galaxy A06 have an AMOLED screen?

No. The Samsung Galaxy A06 has a 6.7-inch LCD screen that runs at up to 60 Hz. An LCD glows slightly grey on a black picture in the dark; that is normal for this model.
